## Relevant findings from the submitted WOC dataset

- 600 total transactions; 450 with a receipt, 150 without.
- Of the 150 lacking receipts, 92 (61.3%) were unmatched.
- Of the 450 with receipts, 50 (11.1%) were unmatched.
- Expenses without receipts represented $118,620.44 of $179,361.87 unmatched spending.

Missing supporting documentation was strongly associated with unmatched transactions in this case-study dataset. This does not show causation. Management should investigate process causes and prioritize receipt capture, matching, and exception follow-up.
