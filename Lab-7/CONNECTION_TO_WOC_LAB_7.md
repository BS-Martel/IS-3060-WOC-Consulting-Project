## Connection to accounting track

The submitted LedgerFlow prototype is an **accounts payable** demonstration for a fictional small business, not the same workflow as WOC employee expense reconciliation. It demonstrates transferable accounting process ideas:

1. Centralize invoice fields and status information rather than relying on scattered emails and spreadsheets.
2. Validate required fields and check duplicate supplier/invoice combinations.
3. Make due dates, approval actions, and activity history visible to staff and managers.
4. Provide a structured CSV export for downstream review.

The report documents an iterative process from static explanation (v1), to intake and search (v2), to workflow features (v3). This is a browser-side educational prototype, not an authenticated or server-backed application.
