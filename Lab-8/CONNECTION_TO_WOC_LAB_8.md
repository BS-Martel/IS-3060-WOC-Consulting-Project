# Lab 8 | AI Interaction Testing and Oversight

## Original submitted artifact
- `IS3060_Lab8_Broncos_Final.pdf`: a 21-page report documenting a privately configured Denver Broncos social media assistant, a proposed information-system design, four live tests (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook), observations, and screenshots.

## Scope of the original lab

This was a sports social-media project, not an accounting assistant. The report distinguishes a private assistant plugin from the Custom GPT requested in the original lab instructions; it does not establish that a WOC chatbot has been built.

## Transferable lesson for WOC

The project illustrates why an organizational AI assistant needs structured inputs, source information, documented tests, limits on unverified claims, and human approval. A future **proposed** WOC expense-policy assistant could follow similar design principles to answer questions about documentation or route uncertain cases to accounting staff.

Any WOC-specific bot would require a separately approved knowledge base, controlled access, privacy review, realistic test cases, and an employee escalation path. No such implementation is claimed here.
