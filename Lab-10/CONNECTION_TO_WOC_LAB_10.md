# Lab 10 | AI Strategy, Pilot Design, and Sourcing Decisions

## Original submitted artifact
- `IS3060_Lab_10_Decathlon_Executive_Proposal_Final.docx` (and `.pdf`): a proposal for a Decathlon product-image classification pilot, Teachable Machine screenshots, two preview tests, sourcing analysis, cost assumptions, and governance recommendations.

## Scope of the original lab

The original model classified **athletic shoes** and **sports balls**, not accounting transactions. The report documents 11 shoe training images, 9 ball training images, and 2 correct preview classifications. This tiny sample is feasibility evidence only, not a production accuracy estimate.

## Transferable lesson for the accounting project

Lab 10 shows how to evaluate an AI opportunity without overstating benefits: identify a narrow use case, measure data needs and outcomes, preserve human review, consider privacy and vendor risk, compare sourcing options, and run a limited pilot before committing to deployment.

For the WOC accounting case, these principles could inform a **future evaluation** of expense exception detection or document classification. Such tools have not been built or tested for WOC in this lab. The estimated costs and benefits in the submitted Decathlon proposal should not be presented as WOC cost projections.
