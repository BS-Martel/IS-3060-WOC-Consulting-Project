## Executive recommendation

Pilot LedgerFlow to standardize receipt and invoice capture, flag exceptions, prevent undocumented approvals and improve reconciliation visibility. Validate security and select a production platform before real-data use.

The Lab 2 Tableau workbook contains 600 expenses. Of those, 150 lacked receipts (25%), 142 were unmatched (23.7%), and 83 were pending (13.8%). Of the expenses without receipts, 92/150 were unmatched (61.3%) versus 50/450 (11.1%) with receipts. Total unmatched spending was $179,361.87. These are descriptive case-data results, not proven financial loss or causation.
